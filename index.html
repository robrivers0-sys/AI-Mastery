Your Weekly Routine

1–3 Hours Done Right

The key is consistency, not intensity. Here's exactly how to structure your limited time each week so that every session compounds on the last.

⏱️
The 3-Session Rule: Aim for 3 × 45-minute focused sessions per week. Phone down, notifications off. One topic per session. AI learning compounds faster than almost anything — 45 minutes of deliberate practice beats 3 hours of distracted watching every single time.
Session 1 — Learn
Study a concept
Watch a tutorial, read a guide, or work through official documentation for your current phase
45 min
Session 2 — Build
Make something real
Apply what you learned — build an automation, a prompt, a workflow, or a small tool
45–60 min
Session 3 — Share
Document & publish
Write a LinkedIn post, share in a community, or document what you built for your portfolio
30 min
The Build-in-Public Advantage

Document your journey publicly on LinkedIn. Post weekly about what you're learning, what you've built, and what's working. In the UK AI space, sharing your learning IS a business strategy — clients find you, collaborators reach out, and your skills become provably real. Success Principles

The Mindset That Wins

Technical skill is only half the equation. These six principles separate people who talk about AI from people who build real businesses from it.

🎯

Niche Before Scaling

Pick one type of client (e.g. dental clinics in South London) and become the AI expert for them. Generalists struggle — specialists command premium prices and get referrals automatically.

🔁

Consistency Over Intensity

Three focused sessions per week beats one exhausting 8-hour weekend. AI compounds through repetition. Your 1–3 hours per week is genuinely enough — if you protect it from distractions.

💸

Charge Early, Not Later

Don't wait to feel "ready." Try to charge your first client by Month 5. Real feedback from real money teaches you 10× faster than any course, and you'll never feel fully ready without it.

🧪

Build, Don't Just Plan

Every week, ship something real. Not a plan to build something — the actual thing. AI tools mean your MVP can go live in hours, not months. Speed of experimentation is your biggest advantage.

🌐

Local Is a Goldmine

Croydon and Greater London have thousands of SMEs that know they need AI but have no idea where to start. Five local retainer clients at £600/month is £3,600/month — and they're right on your doorstep.

📰

Stay Current, Daily

AI moves faster than any technology in history. Spend 10 minutes each morning on TLDR AI, The Rundown AI, or Ben's Bites newsletters. Awareness of new tools is a genuine competitive moat.
